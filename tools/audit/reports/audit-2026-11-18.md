Read-only audit of every record on the site. **No record was changed or deleted.** Findings are split into **verified facts** (counted from the data and from official sources) and **estimates / judgment calls** (labeled).

**Why "outdated" is 0:** the freshness clock starts at the date each county list was last copied (the `checked` date in the file). All lists were copied in the last 7 to 16 days. This measures how recently WE checked, not whether the county has changed its list since.

## 8. What this audit did NOT verify (honest limits)
* **Amounts, interest rates, auction dates and zoning were not independently verified.** They are copied from each county's official list. "Verified" above means parcel ID, owner and acreage cross-checks only.
* **Zoning:** the records have a land-use field (not zoning) where the county provides it; 13,301 records have none.
* **Only 30 counties in 9 states have parcel-level data.** The other states and counties have state-guide links only; no parcel records exist to audit.
* **Connection checks cover the county pages, not the data.** 20 pages refused or failed from this environment.
* **Freshness is how recently we copied the list, not whether the county changed it.**
