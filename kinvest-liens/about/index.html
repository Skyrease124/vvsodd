Who it’s for

Beginners and self-directed investors who want to learn how tax liens work, compare states and shortlist liens to research.

Where our information comes from

State rules from statutes and investor-education sources, checked against each other. County lists straight from official county files, with source and date checked. If we can’t confirm something, we say so.

What PricePocketAsset is not. Education and research tools only, not legal, tax or investment advice. We don’t sell liens, hold your money, or tell you to buy or bid. Tax lien investing involves risk, including loss of principal. Always verify with the county before you bid.
