Billing, Subscription and Refund Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026 (version 2026-11-18). This is a plain-language policy written to match how the site actually works. It is not legal advice and should be reviewed by a lawyer before the site is promoted widely.

This page explains how payments work on PricePocketAsset. It applies to the PricePocketPro membership. Optional donations have their own page: Donation terms. It is part of our Terms of Use.

Who you are paying, and how

PricePocketAsset (“we”, “us”) runs this website. Payments are handled by Stripe, an independent payment processor. You enter your card or other payment details on Stripe’s secure Checkout page, not on our site. We never see or store your full card number or security code. Stripe’s own privacy policy and terms apply to the payment itself.

1. Payment authorization

  • You may pay only with a card or other payment method that you own, or that the owner has given you permission to use.
  • Stolen, borrowed-without-permission or otherwise unauthorized payment methods, and any fraudulent activity (including repeated attempts to test cards), are prohibited.
  • We may decline or cancel a payment, review suspicious activity, ask you to confirm your identity or your payment, and suspend or close an account when reasonably necessary to prevent fraud or abuse or to protect other people. If we suspend an account because of a payment problem, we will explain what happened and how to contact us.

Your consent. Before we send you to Stripe, the Account page shows these terms and asks you to tick a box agreeing to them and authorizing weekly recurring charges. We do not start checkout until you do. Stripe’s Checkout page repeats the price and the weekly billing.

Failed payments. If a weekly payment fails, your access continues only through the last week you paid for. Stripe may try the payment again. You can update your payment method under Account → Manage subscription.

Price changes. We will give you notice before the price of your existing subscription changes, and you can cancel before the change applies.

3. How to cancel

  1. Sign in and open Account.
  2. Tap Manage subscription (it opens Stripe’s billing page).
  3. Choose Cancel plan and confirm.

You do not need to contact us to cancel. When cancellation takes effect: cancelling stops all future charges. You keep PricePocketPro until the end of the week you have already paid for, and the Account page shows that end date. Your free account stays open. If you cannot cancel for any reason, contact billing support and we will cancel it for you.

4. Refunds

A weekly payment pays for access during that week. When you cancel we do not refund the part of the paid week that has already started, because you keep access until it ends. We will refund:

  • a charge we made by mistake (for example a duplicate charge, the wrong amount, or a charge after you cancelled before the renewal date);
  • a payment that was made without the cardholder’s authorization, once we have confirmed it; and
  • anything the law, Stripe’s rules or your card network’s rules require us to refund.

We may also refund other payments at our discretion. To ask, contact billing support as soon as you notice the problem and tell us the email on your account and the charge date. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method. Stripe usually shows them within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank.

5. Billing problems and disputes

If something looks wrong, please contact billing support first — most problems can be fixed quickly. This does not limit your rights. You may always contact your bank or card issuer about a charge, including to dispute an unauthorized charge, and nothing in this policy asks you to wait or to give that up. If a charge is disputed we may share records of the payment, the account and the consent you gave with Stripe and your bank to answer the dispute, and we may pause access to the related account while it is reviewed.

6. Our responsibility

  • We use Stripe for payment processing and take reasonable fraud-prevention steps, such as Stripe’s fraud screening, limits on repeated attempts, and 3D Secure authentication when the bank or Stripe asks for it. These steps reduce risk. We cannot guarantee that every unauthorized or fraudulent transaction will be detected or prevented.
  • Stripe is a separate company. It is responsible for its own services under its own terms; this page does not say that Stripe or anyone else takes on our responsibilities to you.
  • Nothing in this policy takes away any right you have under consumer-protection or payment laws, your card network’s rules, or your right to dispute a charge with your bank. To the extent the law allows, our liability for a billing problem is limited to refunding the amounts that were charged in error; this does not limit any liability that cannot legally be limited.

7. Records and privacy

We keep: your account email; your subscription status and dates; the Stripe customer reference; and a record of the policy version you accepted, when you accepted it, and that you authorized recurring billing. Stripe keeps your payment details, receipts and invoices; you can see invoices under Manage subscription. We do not store card numbers or security codes. We keep these records while your account exists and afterward only as long as needed for accounting, tax, dispute and legal reasons. See our Privacy Policy.
