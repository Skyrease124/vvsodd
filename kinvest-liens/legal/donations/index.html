Donation Terms

Last updated: November 18, 2026 (version 2026-11-18). This is a plain-language policy written to match how the site actually works. It is not legal advice and should be reviewed by a lawyer before the site is promoted widely.

We accept optional donations from people who want to support the website. These terms apply only to donations made on the donation page. The PricePocketPro membership is separate and is covered by the Billing, Subscription and Refund Policy.

Optional and one time

  • A donation is completely optional. You never need to donate to use the site, and donating does not change your access to anything.
  • Each donation is a single, one-time payment. We do not set up recurring donations, and a donation never turns into a subscription.
  • The amount can be any amount from $1 to $500 US dollars.

Who receives the money, and what it is for

Donations are received by the owner and operator of PricePocketAsset, through Stripe. They are general support for the website: they help cover the cost of running it (hosting, data, software and services) and of building and maintaining it. We do not promise that a donation will be used for a particular project, and donating gives you no ownership, voting or other rights.

Tax status

We have not verified any tax-exempt or tax-deductible status for donations. Donations are not described or offered as tax-deductible, and you should not treat them as deductible. If that ever changes we will say so here and on the donation page.

Refunds

Donations are voluntary and are generally not refundable. We will refund a donation that was made by mistake or in error (for example a duplicate donation or a wrong amount) or without the cardholder’s authorization, and anything the law or Stripe’s or your card network’s rules require. Contact billing support as soon as you notice the problem. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method, usually within 5 to 10 business days. You can always contact your bank or card issuer about a charge you did not authorize; nothing here limits that right.

Payment authorization and security

Use only a card or payment method you own or have permission to use. We may decline or reverse a donation and investigate suspicious activity. Payment is processed by Stripe on its secure Checkout page; we never see or store your full card number or security code. We use reasonable fraud-prevention measures but cannot guarantee that every unauthorized transaction will be detected. Nothing here limits any right you have that cannot legally be limited.

Records

Stripe sends you a payment receipt and keeps the payment record. We keep the donation amount and date, the policy version shown to you, and the time you confirmed the donation was optional, for accounting and dispute purposes. We do not publish donor names.
