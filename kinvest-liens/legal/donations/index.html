Support Terms

Last updated: November 18, 2026 (version 2026-11-18). This is a plain-language policy written to match how the site actually works. It is not legal advice and should be reviewed by a lawyer before the site is promoted widely.

We accept optional, voluntary contributions from people who want to support the development of PricePocketAsset and its community. These terms apply only to contributions made on the Support page. The PricePocketPro membership is separate and is covered by the Billing, Subscription and Refund Policy.

Optional and one time

  • A contribution is completely optional. You never need to contribute to use the site, and contributing does not change your access to anything.
  • Each contribution is a single, one-time payment. We do not set up recurring contributions, and a contribution never turns into a subscription.
  • The amount can be any amount from $1 to $500 US dollars.
  • Contributions do not provide products, services, memberships, special access or other benefits, and none are promised in exchange.

Who receives the money, and what it is for

PricePocketAsset is a for-profit project. Contributions are received by the owner and operator of PricePocketAsset, through Stripe. They help cover the cost of running and building the website: website development, hosting, property data, research tools and future improvements. We do not promise that a contribution will be used for a particular project, and contributing gives you no ownership, voting or other rights.

Tax status

PricePocketAsset is a for-profit project. Contributions are not tax-deductible charitable donations. We have not verified any tax-exempt or tax-deductible status for contributions, and you should not treat a contribution as deductible. If that ever changes, we will say so here and on the Support page.

Refunds

Contributions are voluntary and are generally nonrefundable, subject to applicable law, Stripe’s requirements, card network rules, and the refund rules in these Support Terms.

If you made a contribution by mistake (for example a duplicate, or an incorrect amount), or a contribution was made without the cardholder’s authorization, contact billing support as soon as you notice. We review each request. We will refund a contribution where we confirm that it was made in error or without authorization, and where the law, Stripe’s requirements or card network rules require a refund. Refunds are not guaranteed in other cases. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method; how long they take to appear depends on your bank or card issuer.

Reporting an unauthorized charge does not by itself guarantee a refund. Disputes are decided under the rules of Stripe and your card network, and applicable law.

Payment authorization and security

Use only a card or payment method you own or have permission to use. Eligible prepaid Visa, Mastercard, American Express and Discover cards may be accepted through Stripe’s card checkout, subject to issuer and processor approval. Payment is processed by Stripe on its secure Checkout page; we never see or store your full card number or security code. We may decline or reverse a contribution and investigate suspicious activity. We use reasonable fraud-prevention measures but cannot guarantee that every unauthorized transaction will be detected. Nothing here limits any right you have that cannot legally be limited.

Disputes and your rights

If you believe a charge is wrong or unauthorized, please contact billing support first so we can look into it. You can also contact your bank or card issuer. Nothing in these terms limits your right to dispute a charge or any other right under applicable law.

Records

Stripe sends you a payment receipt and keeps the payment record. We keep the contribution amount and date, the policy version shown to you, and the time you confirmed the contribution was optional and that you accepted these terms, for accounting and dispute purposes. We do not publish supporter names.
