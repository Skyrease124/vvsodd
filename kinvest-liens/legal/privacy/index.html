Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026. This is a plain-language draft and is not legal advice. It should be reviewed by a lawyer before the site is promoted widely.

What we collect

Sign-up form. If you join the list, you give us your name, email address and (optionally) what you are interested in. We use it to send guides, tool updates and launch news, and to answer you. We do not sell it.

Hosting logs. Our host, Netlify, automatically records technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request. This is used to run and secure the site.

We do not currently use advertising or analytics cookies. If that changes, we will update this page.

The question box

If you use the “Ask a question” box on a state page, your question and the state you are viewing are sent to our server and then to NVIDIA’s AI service to write the answer. Please do not type personal information, names or addresses. We do not save the questions or answers on our site. NVIDIA and our host may keep technical logs under their own policies.

Your state and location

To show your state first, the home page makes a rough guess from your internet connection. It does not ask your permission and it is not saved. If you tap “Use my exact location,” your browser asks permission, and we send those coordinates once to the U.S. Census Bureau’s public lookup to find your state. We do not store or log your location. The state you choose is remembered only in your own browser, so the site can show it next time.

Other services your browser contacts

Some features load content from other providers, which see your IP address and browser details when that happens:

  • Lot maps (Show on map): the county's public map service (ArcGIS), OpenStreetMap and Esri map tiles, and the Leaflet map library from cdnjs.cloudflare.com. They load only when you open a map.
  • Links to other sites (county websites and Google search links): once you leave our site, that site's own privacy policy applies.

Property information

Lien pages show information copied from official county records (parcel numbers, amounts, dates, property use and value). We do not publish owner names or addresses. If you believe something about you is shown in error, use the sign-up form on the home page to contact us and we will review it.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete your sign-up information, or to stop emailing you, at any time through the contact form on the home page. Every email will include a way to unsubscribe.

Children

The site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
