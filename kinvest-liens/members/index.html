Family access codes

Make a code and give it to one family member or a friend. When they type it (when they sign up, or on My account), everything is free for them: the AI, the screener, all of it. Each code works one time. You can make as many as you like.

“Paid through” is a date you type in by hand for now. Payments are not connected yet, so nothing here charges anyone. Turning someone off signs them out right away and blocks sign-in until you turn them back on.
