How we rank

Start at 50. Add points for a county land value many times the tax debt, a buildable property type, a lot of 0.1 to 5 acres and a small debt. Subtract points for lots valued under the debt, road, utility and water strips, tiny slivers, government land, homesteads and many unpaid years. The county value is an assessment, not a sale price, and can be wrong.
