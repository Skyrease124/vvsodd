    About

    PricePocketAsset is an educational website that helps people understand and research tax lien and tax sale investing in all 50 states and DC. Explore the map, read each state’s rules and sources, look up parcels on county sites, browse real lien lists with lot maps, and use the calculators. Members ($5.50 a week) also get an AI question box and an AI screener that points out liens worth a closer look.
